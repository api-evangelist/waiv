# Waiv

> Waiv is a Paris-based AI precision-testing company spun out of Owkin (formerly Owkin Dx) that builds clinically validated, AI-powered diagnostic tests for oncology and digital pathology. Its tests read routine H&E whole-slide images to accelerate biomarker detection, outcome prediction and treatment-response assessment, and are delivered through Destra, an AI-native digital pathology platform.

## API status

Waiv advertises a **Destra API** — "Integrate Waiv's best-in-class AI tests to your IMS with Destra
API" — on the Destra product page. The call to action links to a general sales contact form. As of
2026-08-17 there is **no public developer portal, API reference, quickstart, authentication guide,
OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL specification, SDK, CLI, MCP server, or A2A agent card** on any Waiv host.
Probes of `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/redoc`,
`/graphql`, `/llms.txt` and the full `/.well-known/*` discovery surface on waiv.com all returned 404,
and `api.waiv.com`, `docs.waiv.com`, `developers.waiv.com`, `app.waiv.com` and `portal.waiv.com` do
not resolve. Access to the Destra API is sales-gated.

## Products

- [Destra](https://waiv.com/precision-testing/destra): AI-native digital pathology platform — slide upload and whole-slide-image visualization, AI application launch, interactive result review, case management, and PDF/CSV/JSON result export. IMS/LIS interoperable; compatible with Roche, Proscia, Sectra and Tribun Health systems. Carries the sales-gated Destra API.
- [RlapsRisk BC](https://waiv.com/precision-testing/rlapsrisk): AI test for distant-relapse risk in early breast cancer. CE-IVD marked under EU IVDR.
- [MSIntuit Suite](https://waiv.com/precision-testing/msintuit-suite): AI MSI/dMMR pre-screening from H&E slides across colorectal, gastric and endometrial cancer. MSIntuit CRC is CE-IVD marked under EU IVDR.
- [BRCAura RUO](https://waiv.com/case-studies/brcaura-ruo): research-use-only germline BRCA1/2 pre-screening from HR+/HER2- breast cancer histology.
- [TLS Detect](https://waiv.com/precision-testing/tls-detect): tertiary lymphoid structure detection on H&E/HES slides.
- [Biopharma solutions](https://waiv.com/precision-testing/biopharma-solutions): AI-derived biomarkers for pharmaceutical R&D pipelines.

## Open source

- [wearewaiv/histoboard](https://github.com/wearewaiv/histoboard): first-party interactive dashboard mapping pathology foundation models against literature and official benchmarks (TypeScript frontend + Python scraper). Live at https://wearewaiv.github.io/histoboard/
- [wearewaiv/openslide](https://github.com/wearewaiv/openslide): copy of the upstream OpenSlide C library for reading virtual slide images. Not a Waiv-authored library.
- [wearewaiv/fastslide](https://github.com/wearewaiv/fastslide): fork of NKI-AI/fastslide, a C++ whole-slide-image reader with Python bindings. Not a Waiv-authored library.

pkg.go.dev.
